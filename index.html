About Us

Students' Environment and Hiking Committee (SEHC) is a student-led initiative of the Department of Education and Extension, Savitribai Phule Pune University, dedicated to promoting a culture of outdoor exploration, environmental responsibility and collective action among students Through hiking, nature walks, cleanliness drives, awareness activities and other experiential programmes, the committee aims to help students connect with nature while developing teamwork, leadership and a sense of social responsibility. We believe that exploring the outdoors should go hand in hand with caring for it—and that meaningful change begins when we walk together and act together. 🌿🥾
